CoRide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

CoRide lets Delhi Metro commuters see who else is riding the same line and direction, and chat with people they choose to connect with. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address or contacts. We do not sell your data. We do not track your location in the background.

Advertising

If the app shows ads, they are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings.

Who can see your data

  • Other riders in the same room see your pseudonym, avatar, interest tags and bio.
  • Only people you have connected with can message you or read your chats with them.
  • Our hosting providers process data on our behalf to run the service.

Safety

You can report or block anyone from their profile. Blocked users cannot see or message you. CoRide is for users aged 18 and over. Use of CoRide is also governed by our Terms of Use.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Account & Data Deletion → Delete My Commuter Account. This immediately erases your profile, connections, blocks, chat threads and saved commutes. You can also request deletion without the app — see account deletion.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes and highlight significant changes in the app.
