Delete your CoRide account

CoRide by ZaidBuilds

From the app (instant)

  1. Open CoRide and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Account & Data Deletion.
  3. Tap Delete My Commuter Account and confirm.

Without the app

Email collab.zaidbuilds@gmail.com with the subject "CoRide account deletion" and your CoRide pseudonym. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (pseudonym, avatar, interest tags, bio)
  • Your connections, blocks and pending requests
  • Your chat threads and read markers
  • Your saved commutes and live presence

What is kept

Safety reports involving your account are kept for moderation review. Anonymous usage events are kept for up to 12 months and are not linked to a profile once it is deleted.
